# Code of conduct

Aphrody Labs follows the spirit and the enforcement ladder of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). This page is the short form that applies to every repository, issue, pull request, discussion and review in the organization.

## Our standard

Be precise, be kind, assume good faith. Criticize code, not people.

Expected:

- Respectful, direct, technical communication.
- Credit for other people's work.
- Accepting a maintainer's decision on scope, even when you disagree; fork if you must.
- Using the language of the repository (English by default) so everyone can follow.

Not accepted:

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or slurs, sexualized attention, doxxing, threats.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, spam, or bad-faith bulk issues and pull requests.
- Publishing someone's private information, including credentials.

## Scope

Applies in all project spaces and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, reject contributions and block accounts. Typical order: correction, warning, temporary restriction, permanent block. Report conduct problems to contact@aphrody.com; reports are handled privately and the reporter's identity is not shared without consent.
